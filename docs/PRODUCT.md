# Product

JellyBox is a design-system study by Christopher Robin Fiore. Its visual ancestry is old Macintosh translucency and the colorful, vibrant, alive quality of Frutiger Aero. Its central proposition is that changing jelly density can supply hierarchy, boundaries and grouping while continuity explains ownership and change.

The current proving ground is three independent tools that become connected workspaces. The website uses its own React foundation. A separate component catalog is intentionally outside the site scope. The exported controls are experimental building blocks, not a claim of a complete production toolkit.

Density, light and motion must help understanding rather than obstruct reading or delay input. Text/targets stay stable; semantics, focus and state remain understandable without decorative illumination. Larger adaptive workspaces and media previews are possible future directions; usability benefits still need testing.
