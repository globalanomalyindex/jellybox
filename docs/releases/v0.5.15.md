# JellyBox v0.5.15

Palette choices now remain visible on resting buttons. The shared color rule previously replaced an explicit action pigment with neutral blue until hover; custom pigments now retain a quieter Spectral concentration at rest, gather more on approach, and reach their exact accent on selection. Disabled and unpigmented action controls keep their neutral behavior. The native fallback and optical renderer use the same rule.

The main trio is larger: 110px resting buttons at the established 131% desktop setting, with larger icons and proportional hover pills. Small screens use a bounded 84px resting size at that setting. Compact window controls retain their existing dimensions. Explore no longer has the manual wobble action or its unused impulse state.

Regression checks cover the public color-state contract and actual rendered pigment after choosing Candy and closing the window with the pointer elsewhere. The browser check lives at `tests/browser/palette-propagation.js` and runs through Playwright CLI against an already-open local or deployed study.
