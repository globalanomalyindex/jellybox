# JellyBox v0.5.16

The desktop experiment is larger throughout: 144 px resting buttons at the established 131% size, a 760 px workspace, 56 px header controls, larger type and 48 px slider/value targets. These are actual measured bounds shared with the renderer. Narrow layouts retain their bounded sizes and the inner folder keeps its fixed 204×44 px handle.

A new core `JellyMaterialProvider` carries a workspace's pigment into ordinary controls, dropdowns, selection groups and slider material. Explicit button pigments and success, warning and danger meanings remain independent. The study scopes each tool's content to its own pigment, including outgoing content during switching. Folder clearing and gathered hover now derive their cached Spectral paths from that same owner. Displayed control samples go directly to optics; selection carriers consume their own target, avoiding a second chase through the color transition.

Opening prepares the existing window/control/cast shader variants during idle time and lets framebuffer contrast verification yield during a pill/window morph. Native ink, immediate input, one renderer and the canonical 760/700 ms handoffs remain. In one local headed-Chromium development comparison at 1440×1000, the first opening's longest observed frame interval fell from 633 ms to 133 ms; its 95th percentile fell from 633 ms to 51 ms. The next two openings had maxima of 66 ms and 51 ms. These are bounded observations on this machine, not a device-wide performance guarantee.

Validation: 176 component/model checks, 4 adapter checks, strict consumer types, site/library builds, packed core/optional-renderer consumers and release source hygiene passed. Rendered browser checks cover resting main-button palette commitment, a warm Material slider and folder hover, 1440/1024/390/320 px fit, anchored disclosure, interrupted switching, close cleanup and reduced-motion keyboard input. Hardware, browser and broader pigment/light combinations still need evaluation.
