# JellyBox v0.5.5

Reading, ambient light and density hover now follow one shared contract.

- Native labels keep one intended ink. Built-in transmitting jelly uses black; custom dark skins can explicitly opt into white. Moving highlights verify contrast rather than flipping typography.
- The existing optical front supplies a thinner, quieter reading film across its own contour, preserving colored density at the shoulders. No white label card, outline or extra context. Actual worst sampled ratios and readback failures stay inspectable.
- Shape & optics wakes a small density breath on hover/focus. Thickness, lighting and receiver response move together; the204×44px title/icon/target stay fixed. Motion preferences remove ambient movement.
- The light example is an adjustable ambient desktop lamp. Position stays committed through pointer/focus movement; height, brightness and power work independently.
- The hairline links to the portfolio case study. The portfolio project card opens the live demo directly.

Three experts agreed and cross-reviewed the shared visibility contract, then revised it after native inspection rejected a label-shaped pale patch. Validation:136 component/model tests plus4 static-adapter tests, strict types, site/library builds and packed-consumer checks. Real desktop/mobile browser checks cover the supported composition and actual sampled color/light states. Authored optics and sampled contrast are not a physical simulation or universal accessibility certification. No runtime dependency or commercial font binary was added.
