# Material and density

JellyBox's material is interaction structure. Dense edges establish boundaries and ownership; thin central film supports reading. A child group gathers concentration out of its parent rather than becoming an unrelated outlined card. The current implementation is authored raster optics, not a validated jelly/fluid solver.

## Shared implementation

`packages/react/src/renderer/material.js` defines roles/defaults. `optics.js` uses closed 3D geometry and PBR transmission, a seeded textured receiver, analytic colored filtering and bounded sampled caustics. The study and optional component scene share this family. Core components retain CSS/SVG material when graphics are unavailable or capacity is exceeded.

The study defaults to 6% translucency and 25% surface light. It uses Wobbly 95% movement,131% button size and 17 px spacing, while generic package defaults retain48% movement. The movable light starts at 100% intensity, height 152, softness 45%, warmth 50%; the source defines its captured responsive position.

A native control has steady text, semantics and bounds. Actual jelly can swell, compress or lean toward a nearby pointer. The target never follows it. Selected checks, focus and labels stay usable without the light.

## Parent density

`density-field.js` and `volume-profile.js` supply a filled, continuous field. Thickness, optical normals, concentration, roughness and background receiver filtering share that field. The inner neutral clear-water area has a broad crown, a quiet reading plateau and a defined shoulder. Absorption grows through the whole area, not just a bright annulus. Zero concentration preserves the displayed parent pigment exactly.

Shape & optics has a fixed204×44 px handle. A smooth union and common envelope let the compact pill reach into a fuller folder without a left bulge or a second sheet. Natural content height is measured; disclosure phase is applied once. A bounded local strain supplies the settling stretch/compression, strongest on gather-back. Text/icon/click target do not deform.

Dense inner areas attenuate distracting shadows/caustics behind their reading planes. This is an authored legibility policy, not energy-conserving scattering. Main-window absorption tint floors channels to .2 display sRGB after Spectral sampling so extreme black does not extinguish every thin-film transmission path. That one tint feeds PBR, analytic casts and photons. Primary pigment endpoints remain exact.

## Ownership and color

An independent button joins the upper-left window edge as its tab. Explicit handoff ownership fully retires that separate carrier; distance guesses must not leave a second pill over the title. Primary faces and body content are transparent, with no white contrast card. CSS/forced-color fallbacks remain available.

Spectral.js supplies cached pigment-inspired transitions. It does not simulate colored transparent light. Semantic tones distinguish rest/highlight/selection and save/restore/delete, with stable checks/labels carrying meaning as well. A preview is separate from a committed palette.

## Light, scroll and bounds

The bulb controls a real scene light. Highlight direction, colored casts and sampled flux follow its position/height/softness/warmth. Reading regions quiet receiver artifacts. The shared scene continues through the story using a viewport-bounded scroll frame and 64 px scissor guard.

Receiver sampling remains bounded, but cached fields move with their material every frame. Rebasing the canvas translates the sampled field immediately; it must not wait up to 125 ms for another photon tick. Paper grain keeps its document-space phase.

The optional bottom scroll cue copies only a framebuffer strip plus 24 px guard and runs a 13-tap progressive filter. Its shallow clear-water concentration and crown respond to the same light. It restores renderer state, disposes owned resources and skips at zero strength. Three masked browser backdrop layers soften live DOM text above the canvas. This hybrid does not make WebGL sample HTML and is not a physical jelly slab.

The cue is active only while the story is entering/occupying the viewport. It turns off immediately on returning to the hero and yields to the signature, selected text, focus, forced colors/reduced transparency and document end. Neither it nor the optical canvas intercepts input.

## Limits

One optional scene supports 32 visible registered surfaces and two selection groups. Geometry/flux updates are bounded and cached; overflow retains fallback. Raster transmission, analytic casts and sampled transport are approximate, single-scene techniques. Performance varies by device, browser, resolution and scene size. Verify rendered contrast across relevant light/color states; token contrast is not a universal accessibility result.


## Activity and contrast

A loading pocket circulates around the capsule rim when work is indeterminate, or gathers from one origin when a reported progress value is available. Its normals, pigment and receiver are the same field used for other moving pockets. Success, warning and failure keep the material body and change its semantic pigment; the host owns these states.

Readable ink is a separate native layer. The optional renderer samples display-encoded material pixels into a bounded atlas, then a shared contrast policy chooses dark/light text against the least favorable sampled luminance. A thin opposing glyph halo protects mixed highlights and moving loading pockets. No HTML label image is rendered into WebGL, and a sampled result is not an exhaustive accessibility certification. Standalone story controls use contact casts so a remote light cannot send a decorative footprint through surrounding prose.
